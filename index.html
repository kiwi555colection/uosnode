How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself.

Your device joins as a node, the system orchestrates its spare capacity toward live demand, and you earn for the work it completes.

01

Sign up with X

Connect your X account and add a payout address. Your device joins the network as a node in a couple of taps.

02

Get orchestrated

The orchestrator matches live demand to your node — routing work by location, uptime, and available capacity, automatically.

03

Earn $UOS

Every verified unit of work your node completes earns $UOS, settled straight to your wallet.
